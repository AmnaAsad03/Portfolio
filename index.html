    Published Research Computer Vision · Deep Learning

    Interpreting Silent Speech: Recognizing Hand Sign Language from Images using ViT

    This research investigates the use of Vision Transformers (ViTs) to recognize American Sign Language (ASL) hand signs from images. It applies transformer-based deep learning to visual pattern recognition, focusing on sign classification and model performance evaluation.

    87,030 Dataset Images
    99.91% Reported Accuracy
    0.09 Reported Loss

    Research Highlights

    • Approach: Vision Transformer (ViT) for image-based sign language recognition.
    • Task: Classification of American Sign Language hand signs.
    • Focus: Deep learning, visual pattern recognition, and accessibility.

    Authors
